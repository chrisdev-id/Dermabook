## Indikasi resmi (ringkasan)

- Lipolisis non-invasif abdomen, pinggang, punggung, dan paha (BMI ≤ 30), serta area submental (BMI ≤ 49).
- Dengan petite mask di area submental, juga dapat memengaruhi tampilan jaringan kendur.

Ringkasan di atas disusun dari teks *Indications for Use* dokumen 510(k) K182741. Jika ada perbedaan, yang berlaku adalah teks asli di bawah.

## Teks asli Indications for Use (K182741)

```text
The Cynosure SculpSureTM is intended for non-invasive lipolysis of the abdomen, flanks, back, and thighs in individuals
with a Body Mass Index (BMI) of 30 or less. In addition, the device is intended for non-invasive lipolysis of the
submental area in individuals with a BMI of 49 or less. The device is intended to affect the appearance of visible fat
bulges in the abdomen, flanks, back, thighs and submental area. When using the petite mask for non-invasive lipolysis of
the submental area, the device can also affect the appearance of lax tissue in the submental area.
```
