## Indikasi resmi (ringkasan)

- **595 nm**: lesi vaskular jinak (telangiektasis wajah dan tungkai, rosacea, port-wine stain, hemangioma, angioma, poikiloderma of Civatte), lesi kulit jinak (veruka, bekas luka, striae, psoriasis), kerutan, lesi pigmen epidermal, akne vulgaris inflamasi, serta port-wine stain dan hemangioma pada anak.
- **1.064 nm (Prima)**: lesi vaskular jinak, lesi kulit jinak, dan kerutan periokular/perioral.

Ringkasan di atas disusun dari teks *Indications for Use* dokumen 510(k) K230990. Jika ada perbedaan, yang berlaku adalah teks asli di bawah.

Vbeam Prima:
Vbeam Prima is indicated for the following:
595 nm

- General Surgery:
- Photocoagulation of benign cutaneous vascular lesions and benign cutaneous

lesions.
- Dermatology/Plastic Surgery:

o For treatment of benign cutaneous vascular lesions, such as facial and leg
telangiectasia, rosacea, port wine stains, hemangiomas, angioma, spider
angioma, Poikiloderma of Civatte, and benign cutaneous lesions, such as
warts, scars, striae and Psoriasis and the treatment of wrinkles.

- Treatment of Benign Epidermal Pigmented Lesions.
- Treatment of Inflammatory Acne Vulgaris.
- Gynecology:

o Photocoagulation of benign cutaneous lesions and benign vascular lesions in
gynecology.

- Podiatry:
o Treatment of benign cutaneous lesions, such as warts.

- Pediatric Population
o Treatment of cutaneous capillary malformations, also known as port wine
stains (PWS), and infantile hemangiomas (IH) / congenital hemangiomas

1064 nm

vascular lesions such as, but not limited to, port wine stains, hemangiomas, warts,

solar lentigos (sun spots), café-au-lait macules, seborrheic keratoses, nevi, chloasma,
verrucae, skin tags, and keratoses. The laser is also indicated for the treatment of
wrinkles such as, but not limited to, peri-ocular and peri-oral wrinkles.
