## Indikasi resmi (ringkasan)

- Lipolisis dengan bantuan dingin pada lengan atas, bra fat, punggung, banana roll, paha, abdomen, dan pinggang (BMI ≤ 30).
- Area submental dan submandibular (BMI hingga 46,2). Pada area submental juga dapat memengaruhi tampilan jaringan kendur.
- Mode pendinginan/pemanasan juga untuk mengurangi nyeri pada tindakan dermatologi dan pasca-trauma/operasi.

Ringkasan di atas disusun dari teks *Indications for Use* dokumen 510(k) K233732. Jika ada perbedaan, yang berlaku adalah teks asli di bawah.

## Teks asli Indications for Use (K233732)

```text
The CoolSculpting Elite System is a skin cooling or heating device. It can be used in cooling or
heating mode.

Cooling Mode
* Indicated for cold-assisted lipolysis (breakdown of fat) of the upper arm, bra fat, back fat, banana
roll, thigh, abdomen, and flank (or "love handles") in individuals with a Body Mass Index (BMI) of 30
or less.
* Intended for cold-assisted lipolysis of the submental and submandibular areas in individuals with a
BMI up to 46.2.
*Intended to affect the appearance of visible fat bulges in the upper arm, bra fat, back fat, banana
roll, submental and submandibular areas, thigh, abdomen, and flank.
*When used for cold-assisted lipolysis of the submental area, the device can also affect the
appearance of lax tissue in the submental area.
*Can be used to minimize pain and thermal injury during laser and dermatological treatments.
*Can be used as a local anesthetic for procedures that induce minor local discomfort.

Heating or Cooling Mode
* Can be used to minimize pain post-trauma and post-surgery.
*Can be used to provide temporary relief of minor aches, pains, and muscle spasms.
* The ZELTIQ Pretreatment Skin Wipe and gel pad facilitate thermal contact of the device with a
patient's skin by mitigating minor variances in device-to-skin contact.
```
